# Fresh full-section inventories: evidence improvement, semantic limits remain

Two fresh calls compared current quotation inventory with the frozen CUE passage-ID
inventory on the original full-section source. Both prompts include identical
current CUE complete-meaning/example guidance. Medium thinking, temperature zero,
no numeric thinking budget or application output cap; no retries or repairs.

Quote: 22 raw, 16 accepted including one background, six reference refusals;
8,424 tokens. Passage: 20 raw, all accepted including one background, zero refusals;
5,593 tokens. Both STOP and replay identically. Total 14,017 tokens.

Quote failures include rewritten newlines, spliced non-contiguous quotations and
ambiguous (3), (2), (d) scope markers. Passage selections resolve to relevant
supplied paragraphs/ranges. Different segmentation means counts are not recall.

Initial meaning review hid arm labels and evidence-format fields, then revealed
the key and inspected complete evidence. Both retain teacher scope but omit teacher
details/reference and eligibility-notice reference. Both have uniformity/modality
weaknesses. Passage retains combined accounting exceptions, whereas quote loses
them in a separate accounting permission. Passage misclassifies the uniformity
must-duty as condition. These are revisable same-agent observations.

Decision: bounded evidence-selection improvement; broad clean-meaning criterion
not established. Next integrate only the existing CUE passage-ID inventory and
resolver, retaining shared current example guidance, refusal behavior and provenance.
Treat adoption as an evidence improvement, not full semantic approval. Run focused
schema, evidence and replay checks. No grouping instruction or new Core type.

This comparison has one call per arm on known development data. Schema metadata,
constraints, enum order and evidence instructions differ; do not attribute effects
solely to ID syntax. No production integration was performed. Local and uncommitted.
