# Example inheritance: narrow comparison passes

Eight medium-thinking, temperature-zero source-inventory calls compared the
unchanged passage-ID prototype schema with a single added instruction: standalone
examples inherit their governing rule's conditions unless the source overrides
that scope. The failed generic reminder was not included.

Original teacher paragraph: control 0/2 condition-complete; treatment 2/2.
Synthetic explicit scope override: both arms 2/2 correct. The original fixture
and its context are unchanged. The synthetic variant changes only the teacher
sentence's opening and is labeled invented. No new substantive treatment error
was observed. Original control repeat 2 additionally omitted outside-classroom/home
work; treatment retained it.

All eight STOP, resolve evidence, and replay identically. Total reported tokens:
26,241. Raw source, schemas, requests, responses, inventories, pinned criteria,
review and verification are preserved in the experiment directory.

Decision: the predeclared narrow gate passes. This supports explicitly preserving
the relationship between an example and its governing rule. It does not prove
the internal failure mechanism or generalize beyond this paragraph and the
explicit override. Production remains unchanged; the passage-ID prototype's
other adoption gates remain open.
