# Independent raw-data reviews: useful discovery, incomplete applicability

Three reviewers received no conversation history or prior conclusions. They
recorded source/output observations before consulting interpretations, and all
report that they avoided earlier assessment files. They inspected complementary
captured sources and traced requests, raw responses, decoded records, challenges,
review history and final graph/discovery output. No reviewer made provider calls
or production changes. Their opinions are revisable readings, not gold labels.

- [Reviewer A](2026-09-10-blind-process-a.md): mobile phones and railroad rules,
  final export visibility, audit labels, graph granularity and process cost.
- [Reviewer B](2026-09-10-blind-process-b.md): complete alcohol/railroad workflow,
  source fidelity, every evidence offset, review history and repeated audit work.
- [Reviewer C](2026-09-10-blind-process-c.md): refrigerant/seatbelt applicability,
  competing summary/component readings, actor evidence and standalone statements.

## Agreement

The base extraction retains substantial meaning: dates, thresholds, alternatives,
exceptions and actor roles appear in readable statements with traceable source.
That is useful for source-backed discovery and preliminary human review. Preserved
raw responses, exact source offsets, pending reviews and draft graph assertions are
strong foundations. These samples do not establish retrieval/tagging accuracy.

The main remaining problem is how meaning travels between records. A stopping
exception targets a combined stop/listen/look/ascertain statement without naming
the affected action. A standalone prohibition can omit an exception preserved in
another record. Some child duties lack enough inherited context to be safely used
as isolated search snippets or workflow steps. Reference resolution is not a scope
decision, and a challenge of proposed changes does not test missing proposals.

All reviewers consider the optional process expensive relative to demonstrated
value on these samples. Repeated inventories and re-emission of unchanged records
add cost. Audit `correct` labels can be read as stronger assurance than absent
structure or unresolved references warrant. Rejected proposals and disputes can
remain in run artifacts without appearing in final discovery issues.

## Differences and uncertainty

Reviewer B found no broad loss of meaning in the alcohol/railroad base records;
reviewer C found consequential omissions when refrigerant and child-label records
are read alone. These assessments use different sources and distinguish section-wide
retention from standalone completeness; they are not contradictory quality scores.

Seatbelt child-restraint clauses contain `Notwithstanding any other requirement`
while the section exclusion says `Unless otherwise stated`. The exact extent of
inherited exclusions needs interpretation. Missing explicit applicability context
is observable, but blindly linking every exclusion to every child rule would not
be a justified fix. The mobile-phone carrier exception is similarly disputed;
the clear finding is that its rejection also discards a separately supported driver
edge, not that either legal interpretation is established.

## Practical response

The smaller existing-exemption link format is now implemented locally and verified:
it directly addresses repeated unchanged output while preserving normal source
challenge and review. It does not settle any scope issue identified above.

Prioritize future work around (1) carrying known uncertainty into discovery,
(2) identifying the particular action a qualification affects, and (3) measuring
the benefit of optional review stages against actual discovery or reviewer tasks.
Check existing Core/evidence/application fields before proposing new structures.
Keep these as separately tested changes. No additional production changes were
made in response to the reviews during this task.
