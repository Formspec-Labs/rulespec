# Example inheritance transfers; deadline scope remains incomplete

Twelve calls tested the unchanged successful example instruction on three new,
pre-pinned synthetic passages. They cover a conditional duty with an exemption,
a conditional recommendation, and descriptive possibility. This is a source-inventory
test of the frozen passage-ID prototype, not a full extraction evaluation.

The revised wording retains the workshop example's condition and exemption in 2/2
runs versus control 0/2, and the spreadsheet example's feasibility condition in
2/2 versus control 1/2. Both versions preserve descriptive possibility without
inventing permission or duties in 2/2 runs. Recommendations remain recommendations.

The complete transfer gate does not pass: treatment repeat 2's independent deadline
retains ten working days but not the non-exempt applicability of the underlying
duty. Treatment repeat 1 keeps that applicability; both controls omit it. This
uses the pinned standalone-unit criterion. Conditions survive elsewhere in the
inventory and source evidence, but independent use of the deadline loses them.
Treatment repeat 1 also has ambiguous exemption actor wording; this is not scored
as a confirmed role reversal.

All 12 STOP, resolve evidence, and replay identically. Total tokens: 16,594.
All 40 raw unit meanings and revisable assessments are saved. Production unchanged;
no new calls beyond the 12 or implementation changes. Local and uncommitted.

Next proposed comparison: retain a dependent deadline with its governing duty
versus split it independently, reusing the existing CUE-derived unit structure.
Preserve the successful example wording as a candidate, without claiming a
complete scope fix or promoting the whole prototype.
