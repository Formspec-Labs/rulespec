## Initial findings recorded before implementation review

1. **Exact evidence can attach an unsupported attribution.** In N1, all 12 raw candidates propose claimant text `Department of State` supported by quote `8 FAM 403.1-4`. The name is absent from `document.json#/text`. Eleven accepted units report `component_evidence_unresolved` for `claimants:0`, but accepted unit 2 has no claimant issue. Its graph contains one `rkaf:SourceClaimant` with `rkaf:claimantText: Department of State`, `rkaf:claimantAttribution: rkaf:claimantIsDocumentIssuer`, and an attributed fragment at source offsets 712:725. That span is the prefix of the cross-reference `8 FAM 403.1-4(C)`, not a statement identifying the issuer. The attribution may be externally true; this source span does not establish it. This is a confirmed source/evidence mismatch, not merely a missing field. Why only one survives requires implementation review.
2. **Meaning is present where standalone units or links are absent.** N2 lacks N1's separate statement `The applicant generally will need the name change documentation to change the name on her/his ID.` However, N2 accepted unit 8 preserves that exact statement in `context_quotes`. Both photo captures preserve `The goal is the best likeness that can reasonably be obtained:` inside scope/context quotes attached to infant units, without making it a standalone statement. Calling either meaning wholly omitted would be wrong. A search or tagger that indexes only summaries could still lose these useful explanations.
3. **The photo replacement trigger has no usable links to its stated requirements.** In P1 and P2 accepted unit 4, `quote` says `You must request a new photograph if the photograph submitted does not meet the following requirements:`. Units 5 and 6 preserve the color and timing/likeness requirements and quote the trigger in `scope_quotes`. All four captures have `relation: none`, `applies_to: []`, and accepted `target_ids: []` throughout. Thus source adjacency and meaning survive, but direct unit-to-unit traversal does not. The source mixes a `must` trigger with a child `should`; it would be unjustified to silently promote the child to `must`.
4. **The exceptions survive as prose but lack traversable exception relationships.** In N1/N2 unit 4, the previous-name prohibition retains `unless the name change documentation specifically permits...` in summary and/or logic/scope. Unit 7 retains both the older-name-change/DS-11 condition and urgent-travel condition in summary and scope. Missing explicit exception edges is a graph/discovery limitation, not proof those qualifications disappeared. The remote married-name exception remains a reference; its contents are absent from the source and should remain unknown.
5. **Structured-data withholding and generation omissions differ.** N2 accepted unit 8 has nonempty `scope_text` but no `scope_quotes`, and reports `scope_evidence_incomplete`; the notation timing still appears in its summary and main source quote. P1 unit 1 reports unresolved `alternative:0` for `white` while `off-white` resolves; both words survive in the main quote and summary. N1 keeps all claimant suggestions in accepted records and serialized meaning, even where specialized claimant evidence is unresolved. Conversely, N2 unit 10 has no typed one-year value, though the condition is present in scope/summary; N1 unit 11 contains that typed value. That is inconsistent generation of a facet, not disappearance of temporal meaning.
6. **Useful semantic treatment is already present.** Both photo captures distinguish `Applicants may wish to re-use...` as `statement/possible`, not permission, and preserve the warning that recent certificate issuance does not prove photograph recency. Names captures preserve the greater-than-one-year and within-one-year conditions and the limited-validity permission. These positives constrain proposed fixes: do not damage them while chasing higher unit counts.
7. **No rejection count demonstrates completeness.** All four have accepted counts 12/11/10/10, zero rejected units, and no extraction refusals. All validation files pass structural checks and explicitly say `semantic_accuracy: Requires independent source review`. Raw `parsed.extractions` counts match candidates, and every raw `unit_attributes` field equals its candidate value. The observed semantic selection differences already exist in raw provider output; candidate conversion did not create them.

These are source-based reviewer observations, not a gold annotation set. Two underlying excerpts and four captures cannot establish a general error rate or retrieval benefit. Initial hypotheses: substring-only evidence matching explains the claimant and `white` asymmetry; a flat unit model explains the unlinked trigger; full candidate serialization explains why unsupported structured suggestions remain searchable in meaning. These hypotheses remain unconfirmed at this checkpoint.

### Two bounded, provider-free probes

Both probes ran in memory with bytecode writes disabled. Neither modified captures or production code.

**Typed-value adversary.** I copied N2 candidate 6, changed only `typed_values[0].value` from `P1Y` to `P2Y`, and compiled it against the original document using `compile_candidates`. The quotation still said `more than one year prior to applying on form DS-11`. Result: one accepted candidate, zero rejected candidates, no issue whose field starts with `typed_values`, and one typed `P2Y` assertion. The probe demonstrates a missing normalization-consistency check. It is not a claim that a captured model actually made this error. `enrichment.py:37-46` validates lexical datatype and that comparator/unit/anchor words occur in the quote; it never derives or compares the number. I did not run full graph conformance on this synthetic probe.

**Structural-parent adversary.** I ran `source_passages` on P1's document. Both `(1) The photograph must be in color;` and the subsequent timing/likeness item receive the infant paragraph `d.` as `parent_id`, even though a new unnumbered lead-in introduces the general replacement-photo list. `documents.py:69-105` retains the prior numbered hierarchy across unmarked paragraphs. The observed initial outputs correctly keep color and timing general, so this is not a demonstrated cause of their semantic errors. It is a demonstrated reason not to turn existing structural parents automatically into legal scope or graph relationships.

## What to preserve and what to reshape

**Keep** immutable source text and offsets, raw responses, candidate suggestions, component issues, distinction between `possible` and permission `may`, complete condition wording, refusal provenance, and the separation of source attribution from extraction provenance. The captured graphs mark AI assertions `rkaf:reviewQueueOnly` and `rkaf:draft` (`core.py:347-350`). Those labels constrain downstream use; this report does not recommend bypassing them.

**Reshape** the search-facing interpretation and component admission checks before expanding prompt length. Exact location is a necessary check, not semantic approval. Core already separates `textualEvidence`, `retrievalSignal`, `officialSourceMetadata`, and their evidentiary functions (`constraints/core/evidence-binding.cue`). `constraints/core/source-claimant.cue` defines claimant as what the document attributes, not what an extractor knows. The claimant result violates that intended meaning despite satisfying structural shape.

**Defer** executable logic and aggressive atomization. The source's mixed `must`/`should`, broad `unless`, examples, and external references warrant qualified discovery. Additional unit count is not inherently a user benefit. No reviewed evidence establishes a need for another generic model-review pipeline: inventory/comparison and refinement already exist (`audit.py:337-379`; `refinement.py:358-448`).

## Ranked solution paths

Effort estimates are rough engineering judgments for small local experiments, not delivery promises. Value estimates assume the user's primary outcome is reliable discovery. Each path is materially different; none requires a longer extraction prompt or indiscriminate retries.

| Rank | Path | Probable user value | Small experiment effort | Model/cost effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Search the source and return an evidence packet | High: recover relevant explanations and qualifications already retained | 1–2 days | Zero extraction calls; lexical prototype costs no provider tokens. Future source embeddings add input proportional to source size. |
| 2 | Admit structured components through narrow, type-specific checks | High: prevent misleading authority and timing facets | 1–2 days for attribution and simple durations | Zero provider calls; bounded deterministic work per component. |
| 3 | Discover related units through shared evidence before asserting legal relationships | High for graph navigation; medium for ordinary keyword search | 1–2 days for a reverse evidence index | Zero provider calls for navigation; explicit reviewed legal edges remain optional. |
| 4 | Account for source passages and use controlled changes to expose omissions | Medium/high for reliability, indirect for users | 1–2 days for replay accounting and a small challenge set | Zero calls on saved captures; later counterfactual source tests need one extraction each, only in evaluation. |
| 5 | Map topics at the source/corpus level with an explicit vocabulary | Medium/high when tagging across documents is central | 2–4 days for a bounded vocabulary trial | Zero calls for exact aliases; optional one batched mapping call per document for unresolved mentions. |

### 1. Source-backed search packets

**Mechanism.** Index the immutable source passages alongside accepted summaries. Return the relevant source passage, its accepted units, and their distinct scope/context evidence together. Use a retrieval view; do not alter the meaning assertion or hide unresolved candidate fields. Crucially, do not embed the entire serialized `meaning` JSON as if every nested suggestion were supported: N1's unsupported claimant would then affect search even when no claimant node was emitted.

Reuse `documents.source_passages`, `SourceFragment`, `EvidenceBinding` with `providesContext`/`definesScope`, and the existing source map. Passage identity is a retrieval anchor, not a legal interpretation. The original source can be searched even where AI assertions remain review-only; do not silently promote AI-derived facts to operational authority.

**Smallest test.** Build three temporary in-memory lexical indexes: summaries only; raw passages only; passages plus evidence packets. Use 12 fixed questions chosen from source before looking at rankings: four explanations, four rules/qualifications, four distractors. Examples: “Why return name-change documents?”, “Does a recently issued naturalization certificate establish photo age?”, “What requires a replacement photo?”, “Can every applicant continue using an old name?” Score top-three source relevance, whether the returned packet includes the material qualification, and false authority attribution. Require all four explanatory questions to retrieve supporting source and no loss of a previously retrieved qualification. If raw passages alone perform equally well, keep that simpler result.

**Risk/kill criterion.** Long packets can obscure the answer or overrepresent adjacent branches. Cap presentation length and show evidence roles. Kill the enrichment if it adds no retrieval/qualification gain over raw passages, or materially increases unsupported answers. Twelve questions diagnose these excerpts only; follow with a blind held-out sample before rollout.

### 2. Component-specific admission checks

**Mechanism.** Treat structured fields as separate proposals. Preserve their raw values, but only emit an admitted structured component when its specific check succeeds. For explicit claimant names, require naming/attribution evidence or supplied verified issuer metadata covering the claim. Presence of a name is necessary in the explicit-name case, not sufficient: “Agency X is criticized” does not attribute the document's statement to Agency X. Keep implied claimants unresolved unless separately justified. For simple durations, parse a small declared grammar from exact source wording and compare the normalized value; outside the grammar, preserve the source without automatic normalization. Use token-aware or explicitly selected occurrences for `white`, not looser fuzzy matching.

Reuse `supported_components`, existing issue codes, exact fragments, and source-claimant/evidence types. Do not build a general legal-language verifier. A claimant check must not block preservation of the underlying rule; a failed duration check must not remove temporal wording from search.

**Smallest test.** Replay N1's 12 claimant suggestions plus synthetic examples covering an explicit speaker, issuer metadata, an agency mention, a cross-reference, and genuinely ambiguous attribution. Add a duration matrix retaining the source “one year” while mutating values to `P2Y`, `P365D`, and invalid lexical strings. Require no specialized claimant on the 12 unsupported suggestions, preservation of their original candidate records, acceptance of the explicit supported speaker, correct `P1Y`, and refusal to normalize the mutations automatically. Test direction and anchor changes separately; a correct number with the wrong event is still wrong.

**Risk/kill criterion.** A whitelist can reject real implicit attribution and unusual number wording. This is acceptable only if source discovery remains intact and unresolved reasons are visible. Reject the approach if it begins inventing conversions, deleting suggestions, or using institution-name presence as proof of attribution.

### 3. Graph navigation from shared evidence

**Mechanism.** Build a reverse index from verified source intervals to claims. When a claim's `definesScope` or `providesContext` evidence matches another claim's main source evidence, expose a navigable “related by source context” result. P1/P2 child photo units already quote the replacement trigger; this can connect them without asking the model to recreate a separate condition unit. Use the existing evidence roles and retain direction. Do not label such navigation `exception`, `trigger`, or legal implication merely because spans overlap.

For explicit legal relationships needed later, propose a small qualification through the existing `condition`/`exception` + `applies_to` route and review it; there is no need for a new general graph ontology. Reference labels can remain unresolved resources until separately retrieved with provenance.

**Smallest test.** Starting from the replacement-photo trigger, traverse reverse scope evidence and retrieve the color and timing/likeness units. Starting from the infant eye permission, do not claim that its scope governs general photo timing. Include a source variant with a reordered neighboring independent clause. Require navigation to remain labeled evidence-based, with zero invented legal edges. Separately test the previous-name exception's existing explicit representation from the comparison capture if legal edge navigation is required.

**Risk/kill criterion.** Broad quotations produce noisy related sets; duplicated text can link the wrong occurrence. Require document identity and interval evidence, not plain-string matches alone. The observed `source_passages` parent error rules out blindly using `parent_id` for scope. Kill automatic edge inference if users cannot distinguish contextual association from legal qualification.

### 4. Source accounting plus controlled-change tests

**Mechanism.** Give every source passage a disposition: main unit evidence, scope/context evidence, mixed, or unrepresented. Within long passages, track obvious sentence/list boundaries as accounting aids. A paragraph fully covered by a quotation is not semantically “complete”; the ledger records where text went, not whether the interpretation is correct. Compare captures using these stable source positions so a 12-to-11 unit change reveals “standalone statement became context” rather than “lost one rule.” Reuse the existing source-passage structure and audit accounting, and add the distinction instead of creating another model inventory pass.

**Smallest test.** Replay the four initial captures and require the ledger to classify the missing standalone ID-documentation statement and infant-goal statement as retained context/scope, the claimant as an unsupported interpretation, and absent typed values as absent facets. On a small held-out evaluation set, move an independent paragraph, change `one` to `two`, negate permission, and insert an unrelated institution mention. Freeze expected effects before running extraction: only affected meanings/facets should change. Inspect disagreements rather than treating the original model or reviewer as gold.

**Risk/kill criterion.** Character coverage can become a misleading dashboard. Never aggregate it into “semantic accuracy.” Stop if the ledger encourages forced splitting, rewards duplicate units, or cannot distinguish retained context from newly invented meaning.

### 5. Stable topic mapping from source mentions

**Mechanism.** Extract or locate topic-bearing source spans, map them to an explicitly supplied small vocabulary, and reuse the resulting concept assignments across units. Match topics such as material name changes, identity evidence, photograph likeness, infant photographs, and certificate issuance to stable definitions. Preserve local concepts for unmatched senses, and retain ambiguous mappings. This separates topic retrieval from the model's preference for inventing a fresh rule-title concept on every unit.

Reuse `ConceptAssignment`, `ReferenceResourceRelease`, role distinctions, and the vocabulary snapshot mechanism. Extend the bounded adapter toward source topic spans rather than pretending the existing actor/object exact matcher already does topical mapping. The trial vocabulary is a test instrument, not a new institution-wide taxonomy or automatic RefSpec registration.

**Smallest test.** Freeze 8–12 topic definitions before reviewing mappings; label which exact source passages discuss them. Compare P1 and P2 and one held-out excerpt for topic-query recall, irrelevant assignments, and cross-document aggregation. Require equivalent source passages to be discoverable under the same topic despite different generated summaries. Preserve the difference between “certificate issued recently” and “photo taken recently.”

**Risk/kill criterion.** A shared label can collapse different senses, while excessive local concepts recreate fragmentation. Stop if aggregate topic queries become noisier than source search or if disambiguation requires a new vocabulary-maintenance program disproportionate to the retrieval gain.

## Recommended next experiment and review verdict

**Run the source-backed search packet trial first, with the component-admission matrix as its correctness gate.** These are bounded, provider-free experiments on already available data. They answer whether the current extraction is useful for discovery without first demanding a canonical decomposition of every clause. The admission matrix prevents the trial from rewarding an unsupported claimant or an incorrect normalized value. Do not simultaneously alter prompts, models, unit splitting, and retrieval; that would obscure which change caused a gain.

All four captured requests used temperature `0.0` and one provider candidate. Recorded prompt/output tokens were N1 6,269/7,546; N2 6,252/6,166; P1 3,866/4,664; P2 3,866/3,900, with additional thought tokens recorded in N2/P2. The proposal adds no extraction calls. Dollar estimates would require current provider pricing and are intentionally omitted; no pricing lookup or provider request was needed for this review.

**Verdict: RECONSIDER the extraction-as-complete-index assumption; keep the existing provenance and review structure.** Verified failures concern semantic support checks, inconsistent optional units/facets, and missing navigation. Evidence does not justify declaring all meaning lost, declaring any capture gold, or replacing the extraction engine. Source-backed retrieval and narrow component checks offer the smallest plausible improvement. Whether they improve real user discovery remains unverified until the query trial and held-out review run.
