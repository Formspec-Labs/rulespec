# Same-passage scope hypothesis: completed, inconclusive

Executed the proposed 12-call paired test with medium thinking and temperature
zero. Cases: identical condition/duties in one paragraph, a separate parent,
and an unconditional control. Two repetitions per control/treatment case. Only
meaning.description changes; exact requests verify no other difference.

Both arms succeed on all six runs: required duty meanings, three-year duration
and applicable condition survive, and neither invents a condition for the
unconditional source. All 12 responses STOP, resolve their evidence and replay
identically. Total tokens 21,393 (9,159 control; 12,234 treatment). A few calls
split out a condition/threshold; one labels the introduction background while
retaining its condition in the duties. This is not proof of stable classification.

Decision: inconclusive; do not adopt an extra instruction from this test. It did
not reproduce the earlier omitted teacher condition. The invented source explicitly
says the following requirements apply, making scope clearer than the mixed original
paragraph. Passage IDs with empty scope_refs are demonstrably compatible with
condition-complete meanings; they do not alone explain the earlier failure.

Next informative test, if authorized: reuse the exact original teacher paragraph
and surrounding context with the same single-description intervention and repeats.
No more calls made; production and defaults remain unchanged. No commit.
