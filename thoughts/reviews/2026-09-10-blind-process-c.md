## Assessment

The strongest current product is a source-preserving search and review aid. It produces useful summaries, retains difficult lists, and makes individual interpretations traceable. The examined outputs also contain material applicability omissions. They can support automatic retrieval and suggested tags if the source remains searchable and results carry their draft status. They can supply preliminary evidence for a person designing workflows, but a workflow generator must inspect the whole governing passage and resolve open references; selecting isolated `must` or `must_not` statements is insufficient.

## Concrete findings

**Good meaning preservation is already useful.** Refrigerant extraction preserves ten substitute categories, specific end-uses, the July 15, 2024 addition, and the de minimis alternative between the combined practices and subpart B. Seatbelt extraction preserves the two different label date bands, red lettering, the United Nations/FAA alternatives, the CARES continuation permission, infant age/device restrictions, and sport parachuting purpose. These are substantial retrieval benefits. Grouping alternatives avoids manufacturing one duty per option. Evidence: raw extraction responses and `T/inputs/{refrigerants,seatbelts}/book.json`.

**The promised complete default statement is not consistently complete.** Refrigerant C0000 says venting is prohibited subject to specified substitute exemptions, but omits the de minimis exception in the same supplied source. The separate C0002 exemption and later edge help a graph-aware reader, but the default statement still overstates the rule when retrieved alone. Seatbelt C0007/C0008 label statements say that seats in the date bands `must` bear labels without carrying their aircraft-use context or the section's operation exclusions. Those are realistic bad search snippets and potential false workflow tasks. Evidence: `T/inputs/refrigerants/packet.json` C0000/C0002; `T/inputs/seatbelts/packet.json` C0007/C0008; unchanged statement text in cells 00/01 `discovery.json`.

**The graph adds correct edges but leaves essential scope relationships absent.** Both seatbelt arms link the part 121/125/135 exclusion only to C0000–C0002 and the § 91.105 exclusion only to C0002. The child label and operator duties are separate records, with no semantic parent edge through which those exclusions could reach them. The source says the section or paragraph does not apply, not just the first extracted statement from it. The child restraint permission references the operator requirements, but no target links join that permission to its three duties. The eight-proposal cap does not explain this result: only three proposals were used. Evidence: `T/cells/cell-01/result.json`, `cell-02/result.json`, and their final graphs. The applicable source is in the same packets, so this is not merely an unavailable external-reference problem.

**The challenge step checks proposed changes, not the complete result.** Its schema asks for one judgment per proposal. In these cells it confirms supported edges and never reports the omitted sibling targets. That is consistent with its bounded task, but successful challenge results should not be presented as an end-to-end meaning review. One seatbelt rationale calls C0002 the paragraph's baseline, although that claim represents its main seating mandate rather than every extracted child rule. Evidence: `T/cells/cell-01/challenge/attempt-0000.{request,response}.json` and frozen `application/refinement.py:327`.

**Some duplication creates competing readings.** Refrigerant C0002's full summary preserves the substitute exclusion and the machine condition “whenever refrigerant is removed.” Its `choice_text` abbreviates the conditions and drops both qualifications. A downstream reader using the structured-looking choice text gets a less faithful rule. The proposal protocol also makes the model repeat the entire long substitute statement and all unchanged empty fields merely to add one target. The preservation guard is valuable, but a narrowly defined link change could preserve those fields mechanically. Evidence: `T/cells/cell-00/discovery.json`, `result.json`, and frozen `application/refinement.py:274`.

**Actor support has a usable path, but the actor experiments do not establish actor discovery.** Refrigerant extraction understood a plausible actor, then lost it because normalized whitespace was not an exact quote; `No person ... may` also fails exact quotation. Retaining the raw values is good. In C's role controls, the actor is already fixed as Staff and the prompt says not to change identity. Both quote and passage selection find support. The alcohol short quote `driver` produces null evidence in cell 3; passage selection produces evidence in cell 0; a longer distinctive quote works in followup 2. This demonstrates a locator/copying tradeoff, not whether the model can discover who owes a duty. Evidence: `T/baseline/refrigerants/refusals.json`, `C/inputs/roles.json`, `C/cells/cell-5/actor/attempt-0000.request.json`, and cells 0/3/5/followup-2 results.

**Definitions and topic enrichment remain largely untested here.** Both target inputs have empty term registries and the extraction prompt explicitly excludes topic concepts, normalized values, and effective dates. The meaning of external definitions stays in textual references; the outputs do not establish resolution of “booster-type” under 49 CFR 571.213 or refrigerant classifications. Keeping those unresolved is appropriate. However, empty concepts and terms mean these cells do not demonstrate automatic knowledge-graph entity linking or rich tagging. They demonstrate useful text extraction and a small set of local qualification edges.

**Confidence is mostly honest, but attention signals are poorly prioritized.** `semantic_completeness: not_established`, `pending`, and draft graph flags are good. Refrigerants has 0/20 “processed” passages because one window is partial, even though all five readable statements survived and only two component quotations failed. Seatbelts has 26/26 “processed” despite scope omissions. These counts describe execution, not meaning quality. Meanwhile local `Paragraph (a)(3)` remains a `section_reference_unresolved` issue even after an explicit link to C0002 was added. Remote § 91.105 also remains unresolved, appropriately. A reviewer needs separate categories for source unavailable, location ambiguous, component withheld, and meaning/applicability incomplete. Evidence: cells 00/01 `discovery.json`; frozen `application/discovery.py:70–101`.

## Practical next steps

- Make every search hit bring its governing scope and linked qualifications with it. Keep original source passages indexed so extraction omissions do not remove discoverability. Do not use missing actor or qualification links as hard exclusion filters.
- Prioritize source-to-final applicability review over another broad prompt expansion. Ask concrete cases: does the label task appear for an excluded operation; does the venting answer mention de minimis; do the two child label date bands remain alternatives? These cases expose errors that quote matching cannot.
- Use one canonical complete reading. Derive or clearly label shorter views; do not let `choice_text` silently compete with the summary. Allow link-only edits that reference the existing claim revision and preserve unchanged content in code.
- Present search enrichment and workflow evidence as separate consumer views. Search can use draft summaries and suggested relations immediately. Workflow preparation should show the source, actor evidence, conditions, exceptions, unresolved references, and the specific human decision still needed.
- Keep exact evidence and replay artifacts, but prioritize a compact review queue by likely meaning impact. A missing whole-section exclusion deserves more attention than repeated “pilot” quote ambiguity.

The useful core is small: source, readable meaning, evidence, draft status, and explicit relationships where supported. Most of the additional process earns its place only when it measurably improves retrieval or reduces the human effort needed to find consequential omissions.

## Reconsideration after source-based challenge

The parent reviewer pointed out an interaction that qualifies my graph-applicability finding: the child-restraint permission says “Notwithstanding any other requirement of this chapter,” while the whole-section exclusion begins “Unless otherwise stated.” I agree that the supplied wording does not justify my earlier categorical implication that every child-restraint duty simply inherits the part 121/125/135 exclusion. Determining the reach of that interaction requires explicit interpretation. The absence of such a recorded interpretation is the defensible concern; blindly adding every suggested exclusion edge could introduce a different error.

The separate finding remains concrete: the extracted label statements do not state their aircraft-use context, and the representation does not show how they participate in the child-restraint permission. The workflow risk is therefore incomplete or unresolved applicability, not a verified conclusion that every missing exclusion edge must be added. The original observations and findings above remain intact so this reconsideration is visible.
