# Review before opening the arm key

Reviewed the masked pairs against the same original source and preregistered
criteria. Arm identifiers and capture paths were not opened. Different numbers
of records and different wording make complete blinding impossible. These are
revisable assistant judgments, not gold labels or a general accuracy rate.

## Board control

`boards-1` has three faithful combined records (7–9); `boards-2` has five (0–4).
Both retain elected-official shortage substitution, democratic low-income
representation and residence, the private-board composition, and the public-
organization board OR alternative mechanism. There is no lost board alternative.

`boards-2` records 1–3 put the private-nonprofit applicability in `scope_text`,
while their primary statements refer generically to the tripartite board. The
whole record retains the scope. The primary statement alone is less self-contained
than the explicit 9910(a) referent in `boards-1`. Mark this as a primary-statement
scope weakness, not loss of the condition from all extracted fields.

## Corrective-action control

`correction-1` has seven records; `correction-2` has eleven. Both retain the final
review-finding trigger, appropriate-assistance OR reasons-report branch, 60-day
plan period, 30-day approval/reasons deadline, notice/hearing, unless-corrected
protection, requested federal review and temporary direct-assistance/offset rules.

`correction-2` record 3 isolates the discretionary improvement-plan option but
labels it `requirement`/`must` and says the State "must, at its discretion" allow
it. The discretion and seriousness/time factors are present, so this is not a
confirmed deletion of discretion. It is a misleading standalone modal
classification for an optional action. `correction-1` keeps that option inside
the broader mandatory process, where the overall must label also covers actual
duties. Separation exposes a classification problem that needs checking before
using individual records as obligations.

`correction-2` record 8 improves on `correction-1` record 35 by retaining the
all-necessary-documentation trigger in the standalone 90-day default-finality
statement. The trigger remains elsewhere in `correction-1`, but not in that
statement itself.

## Decision before unmasking

The version represented by `plan-2` is a substantial bounded capture improvement,
but not a completed solution. Its remaining subordinate omissions prevent a full
pass. More split records also require checking primary-statement scope and modal
classification; no general fragmentation rule should be adopted from this test.
There is no evidence here for automatic reference-context injection because that
factor was not changed. Stop this experiment after identity/usage/replay checks.
