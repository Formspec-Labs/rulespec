# Source-based criteria before extraction/checking

All eight complete prepared source excerpts were read manually before provider
calls. Native pointers and imported footnotes are preserved. External statutory
content must not be invented. Select one default statement per context:

| Context | Target and complete available meaning | Wrong-edit control |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy, 5 USC 552a(d) | Agency permits requested access/copy, including an accompanying person upon request; it may require written authorization for discussion in that person's presence. Preserve (d)(5)'s exclusion of information compiled in reasonable anticipation of a civil action/proceeding. Do not import amendment/review deadlines as access conditions. | Make written authorization mandatory in every access request, including unaccompanied requests. |
| Bankruptcy, 11 USC 521(i) | Individual voluntary chapter 7/13 case, missing required (a)(1) information after 45 days, automatic dismissal on day 46; explain available exceptions/procedures in (2)–(4): party request and court order within seven days; justified debtor extension requested within 45 days, no more than 45 additional days; court may decline dismissal on timely trustee motion after notice/hearing with both good-faith attempt at (a)(1)(B)(iv) filing and creditor-interest findings. Do not invent unavailable filing contents or make discretion automatic. | Make the court's permission to decline dismissal mandatory, or omit a required finding while retaining the other. |
| Credit, 15 USC 1681j(a)(1) | Agencies described in 1681a(p)/(w) must supply 1681g disclosures once per 12 months upon consumer request free of charge; for (p) agencies the request must use the centralized source. Do not extend that limitation to (w), or turn agency/regulator implementation duties into consumer obligations. Preserve unavailable definitions/disclosure contents as references. | Impose the centralized-source condition on both (p) and (w) agencies. |
| Inspection, 18 USC 923(g)(1)(B) | Attorney General's authority without the stated cause/warrant covers licensed importer/manufacturer/dealer inventory/records under three alternative grounds. Preserve third-party reasonable criminal inquiry; recordkeeping compliance once per 12 months OR relevant traced-firearm records at any time; and bona fide investigation to determine disposition of particular firearms. | Apply the once-per-12-month limit to every investigative ground, including traced-firearm records. |

Avoid making every disqualifier jointly necessary. | Require all five disqualifiers before treating someone as unqualified. |
| Compensatory time, 29 USC 207(o)(5) | Employer permits a qualifying state/political-subdivision/interstate-agency employee to use authorized accrued time within a reasonable period after request if it does not unduly disrupt agency operations. | Medical exam, 42 USC 12112(d)(3) | Covered entity may require examination after offer and before duties, and may condition offer on results, with ALL entering employees examined regardless of disability, separate/confidential medical records subject to the three specified disclosure exceptions, and results used only consistently with the subchapter. Completeness is evaluated for the explicit independent-use task using the supplied
source, not as a general legal validity finding. The bankruptcy and medical rows
are demanding compound readings. Judge syntactic alternatives and discretion
from the actual source, not from convenient simplified labels. Materially
unsettled scope is recorded before checker calls; do not relabel after seeing
which arm wins. Correct no-ops and deliberately wrong controls protect against
rewarding expansion or optional-field population for its own sake.

Two source cautions: the privacy exclusion limits access under this section; it
does not establish an absolute prohibition on disclosure under other authority.
The jury paragraph's civil-rights-restoration clause has potentially disputable
attachment to the pending-charge alternative; retain its source wording rather
than turning that grammar into an additional asserted legal interpretation.
