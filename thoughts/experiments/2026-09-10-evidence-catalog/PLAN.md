# Lossless quotation catalog versus repeated quotation strings

Decision: can smaller relationship/challenge inputs retain the same useful output
with at least 20% lower recorded input tokens, without a total-cost regression?
This executes the saved refinement-efficiency plan; no adoption or prompt tuning.

Intervention B interns repeated exact source quotations (length at least 80
characters, at least two occurrences) into a packet-local quote-text catalog.
Quote fields refer to catalog keys; IDs, offsets, roles, statements, rationales,
audit history, target aliases and all other values remain intact. This is text
sharing, not merging source identities. Equal text at different positions retains
different position/identity records. Unresolved quotes stay unresolved. The full
focus/context source remains visible. Overlapping different quotations remain
distinct; the earlier interval compactor is reused for coverage/counterexample
checks, not expanded into a new source-addressing system.

A uses current production prompts/packets and schemas. B changes only packet text
encoding plus the short explanation needed to interpret references. Output schemas
and normal decoded string values are unchanged. Challenges use the corresponding
encoding for both source packet and proposed changes. Each arm's proposals can
differ; compare proposal stages separately from complete proposal/challenge costs.

Offline gate before calls: exact recursive reconstruction, unchanged input,
same source coordinates/roles/IDs, invalid and missing reference refusal, nested
quotes, repeated words at distinct offsets, Unicode, adjacent/disjoint spans, and
AND/OR source qualifiers. At least 20% aggregate character reduction on the two
saved relationship proposal prompts is required to spend on the live pilot;
character savings do not establish token savings. Preserve failures; no fitting
thresholds after seeing results.

Cases: frozen original alcohol and railroad relationship packets from the full
end-to-end test, including prior audit data. Fresh source is the contiguous (a)-(b)
excerpt of official 2025 40 CFR 82.154. One shared fresh low-thinking extraction
supplies its draft; no manual edits. Fresh case has no prior audit and both arms
receive the same empty inventory/judgments, keeping the thirteen-call ceiling.
This asymmetry across cases is intentional; never compare costs across cases as
if they were controlled arms. Both arms within a case receive identical information.

Freeze source expectations before extraction: preserve the ten exempt substitutes
AND their listed end uses (propane's date-qualified added end use stays qualified).
The exemptions can qualify the local venting and appliance-service requirements
when supported; do not exempt other refrigerants or unlisted end uses. The de
minimis exception retains good faith and either the full (a)(2)(i) conjunction or
the alternative (a)(2)(ii) subpart-B route. Do not turn those routes into AND, omit
the class-I/II limitation inside the first route, or excuse prohibited knowing
post-recovery releases through a good-faith-recovery exception. Section (b)'s
practices AND certified-equipment prerequisites both survive. Remote source
references remain unresolved if text is absent. These are revisable source-reading
judgments, not legal determinations.

Saved-case expectations: shipment and passenger exceptions qualify possession,
not driver alcohol use; no-stop exemptions qualify stopping, not gear shifts or
sign consent; the alternative reporting deadline does not change employer notice.
Complete grouped meaning is allowed. A target to a grouped baseline must preserve
the modifier's limited effect. Frozen draft records, not constructed substitutes,
exercise actual repeated-evidence cases.

Bound/settings: 13 calls maximum (one extraction plus six proposal calls plus up
to six challenges), 1200 seconds before starting the next call; no retries. Gemini
3.8 Flash, temperature 0; extraction low/16384, proposal and challenge normal
provider-default thinking/32768. Randomize cells with seed 20260914. One sample
per arm/case; no stability or population-rate claim. Apply supported edits only
to isolated copied workspaces; original captures remain immutable.

Gate: offline checks pass; B introduces no semantic/target regressions, lost
qualifications, invalid-string responses or additional destructive edits; aggregate
paired-stage input tokens fall at least 20%; total reported token cost must not
increase. Record omissions in either arm and refusal/challenge/application failures.
Keep mechanical validity, supported target selection, final meaning, duplication,
input/answer/thinking/total tokens and latency separate. If fewer challenges run
because B has fewer valid proposals, do not count that as a successful saving.
Inspect raw and final records with shuffled hidden arm labels; preserve judgments
before revealing labels. Same-agent judgment is partial blinding, not gold.

Stop if preflight fails or after the bounded pilot. No production changes, no
silent retry, no additional model pass enabled, no commit during this experiment.
