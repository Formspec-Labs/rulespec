# Manual semantic review before opening the arm key

The reviewer saw anonymized pairs and compared their explicit draft, inventory,
and judgment text to the pinned source and PRELABELS. Counts and structure reveal
likely arms; this is partial masking. Labels are revisable assistant judgments.
No new draft defects or outcome thresholds are introduced after seeing audits.

## CSBG pair

Neither `csbg-1` nor `csbg-2` flags any of the three prelabelled groups. All 28
claim judgments have no error/unknown dimensions. The relevant content units
are covered, even where their own inventory meaning explicitly names omitted
details. For example, both inventories state information/referrals/case management/
followup consultations. Both comparisons credit C0010, whose explicit meaning
only describes linkages to fill gaps. The same problem affects the immediate/
urgent-needs and urban-intervention/replication detail in C0006.

Both inventories still omit low-income recipients from the meaning corresponding
to (b)(5). That detail is within one source passage; one decision per passage does
not prevent internal compression. The underlying quote retains it.

`csbg-1` inventories every passage, but some substantive meanings change their
role. Its unit at 39507–39642 says activities 'must enable' emergency assistance;
39927–40071 says partnerships 'must document' urban best practices. The source
requires the State plan to describe how supported activities will enable these
outcomes. Recasting that description as a direct outcome mandate is a material
representation concern, even though governing source evidence remains available.
The `csbg-2` equivalents explicitly keep the State-plan description role.
Finer units cannot be accepted merely because they contain more source words.

The five named faithful plan-control groups remain credited in both. `csbg-2`
also flags a missing effective-date note for the 2014 amendment. The supplied
source explicitly states July 1, 2015, and the draft has no equivalent statement.
This is a supported additional historical finding, separate from the primary
requirement targets. `csbg-1` does not flag it. It is not evidence that the more
granular treatment improves detection.

## Fresh IEP pair

Both variants miss I1, I2 and I3. All eight claims and all substantive inventory
units are accepted. Both inventories explicitly retain training/education/
employment/where-appropriate independent living, plus courses of study, but the
comparison says C0000 preserves all components even though those details are
absent from its default statement and optional meaning fields.

Both inventories preserve written parental agreement/consent as a separate
requirement. Neither carries it into its attendance exemption or excusal meaning.
Both comparisons separately approve C0004/C0005 and C0006. This fails the declared
independently-usable-permission criterion while retaining the condition somewhere
in the overall draft. Do not describe the writing requirement as absent everywhere.

`iep-1` creates a separate excusal unit referring only to conditions (I)/(II),
then separate consent/input units. Its granular inventory therefore also contains
a reference-only permission instead of a complete permission. These units can
help locate source text but are not themselves complete rule meanings.

The named faithful IEP controls are not falsely flagged. Both preserve qualified
teacher/team membership, no extra/duplicate information, optional examples,
research-to-extent-practicable and parent-request transition invitation. No
independent duty or mandatory quarterly reporting is demanded by the comparison.

## Fresh local education plan pair

Both variants credit all thirteen faithful draft claims, including four monitoring
components and the named optional/conditional/example controls. No prelabelled
omission existed here; it remains a false-positive control, not a sensitivity win.
Grouping several content components in one faithful draft statement is accepted.
Neither invents missing obligations from unsupplied cross-reference targets.

## Decision before unmasking

No measured improvement in target-defect detection; not solved. Both variants
miss all three CSBG groups and all three fresh IEP checks. Some inventory detail
is better than the preceding historical audit, but both contemporaneous variants
retain it, so it cannot be credited to the checklist. One-passage accounting does
not prevent within-passage omission or scope/role separation. It also creates
extra background/structural decisions and some less faithful standalone meanings.

No false alarm on the named faithful comparison controls. That alone is weak
evidence of discrimination: the same comparisons approve the known errors.
Do not adopt the treatment or add a mandatory audit pass. Further work should
target whether comparison actually tests the full explicit meaning, rather than
another expansion or reformatting of source inventory. Preserve these cases and
stop this experiment at its bound.
