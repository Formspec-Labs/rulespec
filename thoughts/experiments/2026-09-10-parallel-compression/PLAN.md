# Compression with passage-reference challenges

Decision: does lossless quotation sharing retain useful relationship output with
at least 20% fewer input tokens now that both challenge arms use passage refs?
A is committed production prompts. B changes only quote-string encoding through
the existing evidence-catalog/catalog.py plus explanatory input instructions.
No Core/schema/runtime changes. B includes the identical selectable passage catalog.
The instruction to return literal quotations applies to proposal quotation fields;
both challenge outputs use the same production source_refs schema.

Competing explanations: the previous lost railroad edge was a quotation-copying
failure that passage refs eliminate; alternatively compact input can still impair
selection or copying in the proposal stage. A fresh contemporaneous paired run
can detect observed regressions, not establish their cause or a general error rate.

Cases: the frozen rail-crossings relationship packet from the prior end-to-end run,
including existing audit, and untouched 49 CFR 392.82 (2025) official source from
GovInfo. Search of recent experiment/review Markdown found no use of 392.82 or
mobile telephone. One shared low-thinking extraction supplies the fresh draft,
with empty audit inventory. No full audit. Compare within cases only.

Source expectations frozen before extraction: railroad exemptions qualify the
stopping requirement, not unrelated shifting, safe-crossing or sign-consent rules.
The mobile-phone source prohibits both a driver using a hand-held phone while
driving and a carrier allowing OR requiring it. Driving includes momentary stops
but excludes safely halted off-highway/roadside vehicles. The emergency exception
retains necessity and communication with law enforcement OR other emergency
services. It governs the driver prohibition; a carrier link must preserve its
limited emergency purpose and must not invent permission to require drivers to
use a phone. Its applicability to carrier permission is a source-interpretation
uncertainty, not an asserted gold edge. Preserve defined scope and all conditions.
Wrong actors, unqualified emergency permission, and exceptions to unrelated duties
are counterexamples. Freeze alias expectations after extraction, before comparisons.
