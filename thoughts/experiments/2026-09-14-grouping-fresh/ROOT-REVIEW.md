# Parent's manual reading, before independent-review reconciliation

This reading is not blind: the parent knows the arm labels and token counts. It
uses the complete prepared source and the saved default statements, with their
decoded fields. Native replay, actual-schema validation and Core graph checks
are separate mechanical evidence. The independent reviewer receives randomized
unlabeled sets and no cost data. Neither review supplies expert-approved labels.

## Drug records

Both arms retain the substantive wording of the thirteen 211.188(b) contents.
Separate extraction A1 record 3 carries all thirteen in one long statement and
quotation. Grouped B records 4–16 make those contents individually referenceable,
including the inherited batch-record and significant-step context. This is a
real usability gain for constructing a form from the record contents; it increases
output rather than eliminating waste. Neither arm omits the personnel/automated-
equipment alternative or the two investigation triggers in the main duty.

B19–20 identify the investigation instead of A2 records 3–4's unbound "the
investigation." However, their phrase "failure of a batch or component" omits
"to meet any of its specifications." The complete triggering duty and supporting
quote retain it. Thus the reference naming improves, while the standalone reading
still needs precision. This is not proof of a wholly independent rule.

The quality-control unit remains the explicit actor only for review/approval.
No actor is invented for the passive investigation duties. Earlier release timing
does not incorrectly block investigations after distribution. Neither version
extracts a separate historical-amendment statement from these sections.

## Aviation

Both arms preserve the seven substantive clearance/instruction meanings and
their distinct exceptions, notification deadline, report-request condition and
report recipient. B's actor fields are shorter and better suited to filtering:
"Each pilot in command" replaces A's actor value containing the whole trigger.
The trigger is still in B's statement. Empty scope fields in B are not omissions
when the statement itself includes the condition.

Every light-signal mapping is correct in both versions. Separate A2 records 2–13
provide twelve signal/aircraft-setting records; A2 record 1 is only a table pointer.
Grouped B8 expresses all twelve mappings in one long statement. Do not report a
missing table or missing cell. The loss is independently referenceable mappings,
which the frozen A8 criterion expected at least per signal. The batch directive
speaks specifically of independently actionable duties, so its handling of a
descriptive mapping table is a plausible gap in the instruction, not evidence
about the model's hidden reasoning.

A's OMB approval statement is absent in B. It is documentary information, not an
operative aviation duty, and is retained in the input. This is another difference
to report without inflating it into a safety-critical rule omission.

## Health-information safeguards

Both versions preserve the same 24 duties, actor alternatives, named control
groups, required/addressable qualifications, explicit 164.306 dependency,
as-needed and whenever-appropriate limits, and the physical/technical distinctions.
No new concrete substantive loss was found in the grouped statements.

The separate technical output uses the awkward wording "is addressed to implement"
for several addressable specifications while its modality remains must. Grouping
uses "As an addressable implementation specification ... must, in accordance with
164.306" consistently. Both leave external 164.306 unexpanded; the experiment does
not resolve its legal policy. Do not infer that addressable means optional or
claim that all these records are unconditional executable obligations.

The 12.4% total-token reduction is a bounded gain on comparable records, below
the study's aggregate 15% target when considered alone. This is not a new failure
criterion and does not erase the observed benefit.

## CSBG repeat

Both versions retain seventeen records, the four separate monitoring duties,
two training-process duties, permissions and historical committee-name statement.
Grouping improves the local section names in its records 4–7 and 12. Its record
14 names the evaluations under 9914(c); record 15 says "On receiving the evaluation
report submitted by the Secretary," where separate A2 record 8 says only "the
report." The former is clearer, although it does not carry the subsection number
or every part of the evaluation setting into that statement.

The original grouping-specific report regression did not recur. That is evidence
of output variability and an improved observation, not a deterministic repair.
Both still retain local pointers or chapter context in other records. Grouping
does not consistently convert every default statement into independent meaning.

## What changes the decision

Both arms total 69 records, yet thirteen records effectively move between the
drug-list and aviation-table cases. Aggregate counts conceal a substantial change
in how consumers can address information. Input-token savings occur in every
case; output volume moves with segmentation. The source contents generally survive,
but the predeclared requirement for independently referenceable table meanings is
not met by grouping. A clean general production win is therefore not established.

A useful different hypothesis remains: the added granularity instruction may
improve independently usable units even within current separate-section extraction.
Testing that factor alone would avoid attributing its effect to grouping. This is
an untested explanation to preserve, not authorization for another call or a
reason to introduce a planner, mandatory audit or special-case table filter.
