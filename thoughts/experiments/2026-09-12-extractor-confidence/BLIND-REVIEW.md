# Manual source review before revealing arms or confidence

Reviewed all 81 raw extraction rows in the six masked review files against every
supplied source passage. No confidence score, confidence basis, or arm key was
read. These are revisable judgments, not independent expert adjudication. The
visible output style could suggest an arm; masking is not guaranteed blindness.

The primary labels below identify clear substantive defects. Ambiguous modal
classification, container-only statements and unresolved interpretive questions
are labeled uncertain and excluded from primary ranking. This avoids counting
those disputed judgments as definite errors or definite successes. “Faithful”
means no clear source-meaning defect found in this review, not certified readiness
to generate a workflow. Source references remain references; unavailable external
requirements were not inferred. All labels refer to zero-based raw row indices.

- Rows 0–2, 4–6, 8–9 retain the selected meanings: designation, three lead-agency
