## Additional observations, not new preregistered gates

- Packet 1 record 103 separates the appointive-official permission but does not say
  the rule concerns private nonprofit CSBG boards. Neither its default statement
  nor its scope field supplies that governing context. Record 102 has it, but an
  independently retrieved record 103 could be applied too broadly. This is a
  harmful companion record even though the main composition statement is faithful.
- Packet 2 has no accepted poverty-line definition, annual revision duty, or
  revision formula in 9902, whereas packet 1 has records 12–14. Inspect raw
  candidates/refusals after unmasking before attributing this to the model.
- Packet 1's 9907(b)(1) record 43 compresses the list's innovative-program purposes
  (eliminate poverty, promote self-sufficiency, community revitalization), whereas
  packet 2 record 29 preserves them. This is a further compression tradeoff outside
  the preregistered 24 checks.
- Packet 1 record 46 labels an accounting exclusion `exemption/not_required`,
  although the text says the cost shall not be considered administrative. The
  source reading survives, but whether that classification represents absence of
  duty adequately is doubtful. Packet 2 instead combines the exclusion into a
  larger administrative-cap prohibition.
- Packet 1 avoids the earlier focused experiment's `must, at its discretion`
  classification: record 145 is explicitly permission/may. Both current outputs
  retain the all-documentation trigger for the 90-day finality rule.

Verdict before unmasking: packet 1 is materially better for obtaining the thirteen
plan contents and separating duties, but still fails complete independent meaning.
Packet 2's generic list pointer is not an adequate State-plan requirements draft.
No output qualifies for unreviewed workflow use. Avoid extending these judgments
to all historical notes or to general model performance.
