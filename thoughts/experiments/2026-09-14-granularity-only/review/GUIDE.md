# Blind review scope

Each pair has two randomized, unlabeled sets. Set numbering is independent per
pair, including between the two drug-contents repeats. Do not infer the arms or
read their key, settings, plans, metrics, runners or capture paths.

Compare every record and its populated fields against the supplied complete focus
source. Use the copied EXPECTATIONS.md and CSBG-CHECKS.md only for applicable
checks: drug-contents D1–D6; signals A8; clearances A1–A7; technical-safeguards H1
and H8–H14; monitoring C7–C16 and C18. Other sections are not selected in these
single-section cases. In particular, drug-contents does not include the body of
211.192, and technical-safeguards does not include 164.306 or physical safeguards.

Separate these judgments: complete source meaning; independently understandable
default statement; individually referenceable units; classification and component
fields; supporting evidence. A copied list may retain every word while remaining
one record; a split child may lose its inherited target even with a correct quote.
Missing optional fields are not automatically missing meaning. Preserve uncertainty
about external policy, source taxonomy or actor inference.

Review both drug repeats for the thirteen contents, full batch-record/significant-
step framing and the personnel/automation alternative. Check every table mapping
and setting, distinct aviation exceptions and report timing, safeguards' Required/
Addressable qualifications, and monitoring's report/section referents. Do not treat
all counts as desired outputs or historical source metadata as an operative duty.

Write a concise review with per-pair verdicts and exact record numbers/source
quotes for material differences and shared defects. No exhaustive transcription
or new research is needed. Labels are revisable judgments, not gold.
