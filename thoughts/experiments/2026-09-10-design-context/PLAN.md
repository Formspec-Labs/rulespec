# Context-bearing meaning pilot

Decision: Does the existing deterministic source-context selection improve reading of an isolated meaning enough to justify a larger consumer-facing trial?

Hypothesis: Adding exact evidence and current bounded structural/adjacent context will expose qualifications omitted from an isolated meaning. Competing explanation: the necessary context is remote and existing selection cannot supply it. A third risk is mistaken inheritance from a nearby or structural parent.

Arms: A contains the saved statement only. B contains the exact same statement plus its verified existing evidence, source passages selected by `with_context(context_chars=2400)`, and exact source positions. Source structure is explicitly not an assertion of legal inheritance. This is a bundle; no claim can isolate evidence versus adjacent/parent context.

Cases: Actual saved refrigerant prohibition missing its de-minimis qualification; actual saved child-restraint label duty missing aircraft-use context; actual saved child securing duty with notwithstanding/exclusion ambiguity; one explicitly constructed counterexample where structural parent scope does not govern the child. Four questions per case. Refrigerant de-minimis and aircraft-use are known positive opportunities. Independent service duty, manufacture/use distinction, disputed part135 applicability, and constructed noninheritance are negative/ambiguity controls. Frozen expectations are revisable judgments, not gold. No new provider extraction or fresh natural document; this is diagnostic development data plus a constructed control.

Held constant: One completion per arm/case, eight calls total, current Gemini default model, refinement capture helper (temperature0, provider-default thinking, max32768), identical schema/instructions/questions, balanced AB/BA order, no retries or tuning, thirty-minute bound. No model judge; manually read raw responses after capture. Full source is retained for manual reference only and not secretly supplied to A or B.

Decision rule: Investigate a consumer trial only if B improves at least one of the two known positive opportunities without introducing unsupported actor/scope inheritance or suppressing ambiguity. Count missing selected context separately from model comprehension. No production adoption from this pilot. No gain or selector absence means no general solution demonstrated. Increased token costs must be reported. Accuracy is a comprehension proxy, not measured user benefit or human review time.

Replay: Verify frozen source/case/prompt/runtime hashes, identical actual request configurations, response decoding, and exact source positions. Preserve all outputs/refusals, even failure or noncompliance. Stop after eight calls.
