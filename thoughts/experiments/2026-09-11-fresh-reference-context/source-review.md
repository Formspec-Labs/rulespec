# Source review before provider calls

These are revisable expected readings of pinned source, not current-law advice or
gold labels. No model output has been generated for these cases. The complete
selected focus text is saved beside each original XML capture.

## Title 29, chapter 4B: Federal Employment Service

Manually read the complete selected production window, positions 0–23969. It
contains the chapter table of contents, section 49 and its historical notes,
section 49a's definitions, and section 49b's duties and part of its amendment
history. The known located opportunity is the definition's reference to section
49c, outside the focus. This is a definition/context opportunity; it does not
authorize copying every duty from section 49c into the focus.

Expected checks to freeze before calls:

- **Opportunity:** section 49a(7)'s `State agency`, when used without further
  description and except in section 49l–2, refers to the agency designated or
  authorized under section 49c. That target identifies the Governor acting under
  State statute and an agency vested with the powers needed to cooperate with
  the Secretary. Preserve the written exception and the limited defined use when
  making the designation meaning explicit. A bare retained citation is faithful
  baseline output, but does not by itself demonstrate recovery of this extra
  meaning from supplied target text.
- **No false inheritance:** section 49c's acceptance of the chapter for the
  benefits of apportioned appropriations must not become a new condition on
  every Secretary duty or on an individual's right to have information furnished.
  The Governor's designation is not an instruction for the Secretary to create
  the State agency. Keep an unresolved reference to section 49l–2 if its target
  cannot be supplied within the declared budget.
- **Definition limits:** `State` includes the listed States, District of Columbia,
  Puerto Rico, Guam and Virgin Islands; do not add other jurisdictions from memory.
  `Secretary` means Secretary of Labor in this chapter.
- **Complete existing duty:** section 49b(b) retains the requesting agency
  categories, `as appropriate`, the request trigger and all three information
  categories. It is a duty plus express authority, not merely optional sharing.
- **Modality:** section 49b(e) authorizes assistance with national electronic
  tools in consultation with States. It does not require that assistance in every
  case. The tools' possible use is not itself a new mandate.
- **Historical limit:** the 1948 farm-labor authorization is time-bounded through
  June 30, 1949. Do not convert it or the superseded administrative transfers into
  undated current authority. Heading or table-of-contents entries are not duties.

The automatic selection therefore provides both a possible definition gain and
strong negative controls. It is not an independently sampled document-accuracy
benchmark. The target's full text and source coordinates still need their final
label check when the context selection is frozen.

## Title 38, chapter 7: personnel actions and whistleblower provisions

Manually read the complete selected window, positions 71405–95245. It starts with
an effective-date note for section 717 and contains sections 719, 721, 723,
725–728, 731 and the start of 732. The opportunity is section 727(c)'s express
adoption of `senior executive position` from section 713(d), outside the window.

Expected checks to freeze before calls:

- **Opportunity:** the section 727 definition should gain the actual career/other
  covered-individual distinction from section 713(d), rather than merely repeat
  its citation. Preserve the target's appointment references and any unresolved
  further definition; do not invent their contents from outside knowledge.
- **No false inheritance:** section 713(d)'s neighboring `misconduct` definition
  does not make misconduct a prerequisite to section 727's written, signed
  Secretary approval for reassignment. Section 726 defines `senior executive`
  separately, while section 725 defines `political appointee`. Do not merge those
  terms or broaden section 726's appointment categories using section 713(d).
- **Force and conditions:** section 719(a) says the Secretary shall order the
  reduction for the removed employee under its conditions; 719(b) says may for
  the person who leaves before the final removal decision. Preserve final felony
  conviction influencing performance, notice/response and order-deadline branches.
  The source's own section-number spelling is not silently corrected.
- **Similar but different deadlines:** section 721(b)(2)'s appeal decision is
  within 30 business days; section 723(b)(2) says 30 days. Preserve the distinction,
  seven-business-day appeal opportunity, and the five-/15-business-day branches.
- **Reporting versus permission:** section 727(b) retains the June 30/December 31
  reports, purpose and costs, and four allowed cost categories. Listing a cost
  for reporting does not itself authorize expenditure or create an entitlement.
- **Other controls:** section 728 requires knowing misuse and retains all five
  listed forms; its prohibition is additional to other applicable penalties.
  Section 731's first offense is at least 12 days' suspension up to removal;
  the second is removal. Keep notice/response conditions and do not revive the
  historical 14-day response period from amendment notes.

The narrow positive and these negative controls test whether useful remote
definitions can be consumed without treating adjacent definitions or similar
provisions as interchangeable. The section 713(d) target needs its complete
text checked before the final machine-readable labels are frozen.

## Full target review, before calls

Read all 2,397 characters of section 49c and all 879 characters of section 713(d).
The title 29 target includes historical notes: its 1946 transfer and conditional
waiver of property claims do not become conditions of the section 49a definition.
The definition gain concerns Governor designation under State statute and the

The title 38 target requires a more precise label than the initial shorthand
above: **covered individual** includes appointments under 7401(4), but **senior
executive position** in 713(d)(3)(B) names only 7306(a) and 7401(1). The other

5 USC 3132 reference. Do not add 7401(4) to the position definition or collapse
the two appointment branches into a general covered-individual category. This
refinement follows full source review before any model results.

## Title 20, chapter 6A: vending facilities for blind persons

Read the complete focus, positions 0–23797: table of contents, sections 107 and
107a, their historical notes, and a final Transfer of Functions heading. Also
read the complete supplied targets 107d–3 (6,924 characters), 107b(3) (1,035) and
107d–4 (2,193). No further one-hop targets are automatically fetched.

Possible complete context gains, credited only when connected to focus meaning:

- Section 107a(a)(1)'s uniform requirements cover set-aside funds. Section
  107b(3) limits them to necessary amounts and five purposes: equipment maintenance
  and replacement, new equipment, management, fair minimum return, and specified
  retirement/health/leave benefits. Benefits under (E) require a majority vote
  after full information; the amount cannot exceed the reasonable amount the
  Secretary determines. A free-standing context-only rule earns no focus gain.
- Section 107a(a)(6)'s periodic evaluation includes upward mobility and other
  training under 107d–4. That section specifies uniform effective training,
  including on-the-job training through Rehabilitation Act services; upward
  mobility including education/additional training or retraining for all trainees;
  and follow-along services for maximum vocational potential. Preserve the roles
  of Commissioner and State agencies. Do not invent the unseen Act's provisions.
- Section 107(b)(1)'s income assignment refers to 107d–3. Any supplied explanation
  of the allocation must retain the applicable alternatives, percentages and
  exclusions. Merely saying all Federal vending income goes to blind vendors
  contradicts the target. Bare citation retention is faithful but not extra meaning.

Negative and completeness checks:

- Section 107(a) authorizes licensed blind persons; it does not compel each
  person to operate a facility. Section 107(b) gives priority to State-licensed
  blind persons; establishment is subject to feasibility and U.S. interests.
  Adverse-interest limitations need written justification, a binding Secretary
  determination and Federal Register publication with supporting documentation.
- The Secretary of Education's listed duties remain distinct from the
  Commissioner's implementation tasks. Preserve annual surveys versus periodic
  evaluations; do not restore the removed annual report from amendment notes.
  Preserve the literal `Commission` spelling and its editorial footnote as source.
- Section 107a(a)(5)'s other-public-agency alternative applies when the State has
  no agency for the blind. Licensing concerns blind U.S. citizens and stated
  goods/services under applicable health laws. Lottery chances require State-law
  authorization and a State-agency lottery. Do not restore old age-21 or residency
  requirements from historical amendments.
- Section 107a(b)'s licenses are indefinite but may terminate on the licensing
  agency's finding of noncompliance. Preference is for blind persons needing work.
  The 107e blindness definition stays unresolved if omitted by the context budget;
  do not invent a visual-acuity threshold from outside knowledge.
- Section 107a(c)'s location/type selection is authorized with the property
  agency head's approval and subject to section 107 regulations. Do not turn an
  approving authority into the sole responsible actor.
- Section 107a(d)(1)'s post-January-1-1975 building acquisition/occupation limits
  have different existing-site and construction/alteration/renovation branches,
  consultation, Secretary determination and notice to the State agency.
  The (d)(2)(A) insufficient-users exception requires both Secretary and State
  agency determination. The private-building exception needs a pre-lease food
  facility outside the leased area AND proximate substantial direct competition;
  retain the every-effort-to-lease-accommodating-property proviso.
- `Satisfactory site` is defined for subsection (d), not as a general rule about
  every training program or every State property. Section 107a(e)'s agency
  designation requires an approved rehabilitation plan and the cited Act's
  designated agency; external content remains unresolved.
- Income context 107d–3: recipient is the blind licensee on that property, otherwise
  the State agency. After January 1, 1975, direct competition yields 100%; other
  machines yield 50%, or 30% when at least half the hours are outside normal hours.
  Keep the employee-access exclusion from direct competition. Ceiling regulations
  are optional; preserve the January 1, 1974 floor, no ceiling for the specified
  licensee-operated machine facilities, excess-income transfer, State benefit
  vote and remainder/pro-rata rules. The exchange/ships' stores, Veterans Canteen
  Service and noncompeting-location income at most $3,000 exceptions apply to
  (a)/(b)(1). More favorable arrangements remain possible. These exceptions do
  not revoke the general licensed-person authorization or building-site duties.
  The cafeteria-priority regulation has its own individual determination,
  consultation, reasonable-cost and comparable-quality conditions.

These labels assess supplied source and the declared focus; they do not establish
current law, legal applicability beyond that source, or a population accuracy rate.
