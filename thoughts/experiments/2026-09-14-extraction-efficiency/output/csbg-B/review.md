## 0: requirement / must

The chief executive officer of a State desiring to receive a grant or allotment under section 9905 or 9906 of this title shall designate, in an application submitted to the Secretary under subsection (b), an appropriate State agency that complies with the duties under paragraph (2) to act as a lead agency for purposes of carrying out State activities under this chapter.

## 2: requirement / must

The lead agency shall, in conjunction with the development of the State plan as required under subsection (b), hold at least one hearing in the State with sufficient time and statewide distribution of notice of such hearing, to provide to the public an opportunity to comment on the proposed use and distribution of funds to be provided through the grant or allotment under section 9905 or 9906 of this title for the period covered by the State plan.

Actor: The lead agency

modality_quote: "shall"

## 4: requirement / must

In order to be eligible to receive a grant or allotment under section 9905 or 9906 of this title, the State shall hold at least one legislative hearing every 3 years in conjunction with the development of the State plan.

Actor: the State

modality_quote: "shall"

references: ["section 9905", "section 9906"]

## 5: requirement / must

Beginning with fiscal year 2000, to be eligible to receive a grant or allotment under section 9905 or 9906 of this title, a State shall prepare and submit to the Secretary an application and State plan covering a period of not less than 1 fiscal year and not more than 2 fiscal years, submitted not later than 30 days prior to the beginning of the first fiscal year covered by the plan, and containing such information as the Secretary shall require, including the assurances and descriptions specified in paragraphs (1) through (13).

Actor: a State
