## Flight review

Review-04 retains all three simulator requirements, but only as independent
conditional requirements. C0010 actually says the simulator/device **may be used**
provided there is an approved course at a certificated training center. This
states a permission with only one of the three conditions. The comparison calls
it an accurate statement of the condition in (i)(1), while separately approving
the landing-recency and rated-aircraft conditions in C0011/C0012. It does not flag
either missing condition in the standalone permission. Review-01 also passes it.

This supports a more specific process concern: matching a permission to one
necessary condition can obscure whether that condition is sufficient for the
permission. It is an inference from the text and judgments, not a claim about the
model's internal reasoning. Source-backed joint coverage of separate conditions
does not, by itself, establish that C0010 is complete.

The prior label uncertainty for C0001 remains: both inventories attach a generic
exception phrase to the review-content requirements. The source's exceptions
specifically alter training duration; neither comparison demands removal of the
content requirement. Do not turn this ambiguous inventory phrasing into a
confirmed regression. C0000 classification differs between inventory and draft,
but its full hours/exception meaning survives and was not the primary endpoint.
