- saved-label, C0007: actual original model extraction. It reads as a general
  seat-labeling duty rather than a condition of the child-restraint permission.
  scope_quotes contain parts of the hierarchy, but summary/scope_text do not
  retain that purpose. Wider aircraft/operational exclusions and companion/operator
  conditions are in the full source. Flag concrete lost scope, not every sibling
  condition as an independent labeling duty. No manufacturer actor is asserted.
- saved-adulthood, C0000: constructed claim carrying the exact prior model answer
  from cell-02 q3, including 'No.' and broader operator requirements. The main
  evidence is deliberately the actual companion paragraph, shared with the
  faithful control. The source says parent, guardian, or designated attendant;
  this branch does not require an adult. 'Adult' appears in the separate lap-held
  infant option. To pass this control the audit must identify that specific
  unsupported age restriction; generic unsupported-content warnings do not count.
  The broad answer also omits exact companion roles and does not independently
  restate all section limits. Those are distinguishable findings.
- faithful-companion, C0000: constructed statement explicitly says this is one
  requirement within the cited child-restraint permission. It accurately names
  the companion roles, designation and purpose. It makes no adulthood claim and
  does not claim to be a complete list of permission conditions. Demanding that
  it restate every sibling criterion is a false alarm. A lack of mandatory age
  should not be invented as a defect.
