Construct an independently usable reading for each selected statement from the
supplied source. The statement is the default prose meaning in fields.summary;
other fields separately describe its classification, components and evidence.
Preserve the actor where stated, modal force, action, governing conditions,
exceptions, alternatives, thresholds and timing. Definitions and impersonal
statements need not invent an actor. Empty optional component fields are allowed.
A full quotation or another record retaining a condition does not incorporate it
into this statement. Check incoming qualifications as well as outgoing references.
Explain available referenced governing meaning; identify unavailable material
without inventing its contents. A located reference is context, not proof that
it governs this statement. Preserve correct components and exact source evidence.
A permission plus a separate content requirement should state both, without
inventing loss of permission for noncompliance. An exemption must explain the
waived duty and, when needed for independent use, the separate surviving duty;
that surviving duty is not an invented prerequisite for the exemption. Do not
transfer another actor's duties or import unrelated reporting requirements.
An edit must make a needed correction to this reading, its substantive components
or its qualification links. Populating optional components alone is not a repair
of missing governing meaning. Leave an independently complete reading unchanged.
Report unresolved meaning when the supplied source cannot establish it.
