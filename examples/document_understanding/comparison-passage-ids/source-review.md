# Source review criteria

Revisable source-based labels, not absolute answers.

## C0000

When the approximate timing of the need for leave is not foreseeable, an employee must provide notice to the employer as soon as practicable under the facts and circumstances of the particular case.

Scope: When the approximate timing of the need for leave is not foreseeable

## C0002

Notice of unforeseeable leave may be given by the employee's spokesperson (such as a spouse, adult family member, or other responsible party) if the employee is unable to do so personally.

Scope: If the employee is unable to personally give notice of unforeseeable leave

## C0003

If an employee's child has a severe asthma attack and the employee takes the child to the emergency room, the employee would not be required to leave his or her child in order to report the absence while the child is receiving emergency treatment.

Scope: When an employee's child has a severe asthma attack, the employee takes the child to the emergency room, and the child is receiving emergency treatment

## C0006

Depending on the situation, the information provided may include that a condition renders the employee unable to perform job functions; that the employee is pregnant or has been hospitalized overnight; whether the employee or family member is under continuing care of a health care provider; if leave is due to a qualifying exigency, that a military member is on covered active duty or call to covered active duty status (or notified of an impending call or order), that leave is for a reason in § 825.126(b), and the anticipated duration of absence; or if leave is for a family member, that the condition renders them unable to perform daily activities or that they are a covered servicemember with a serious injury or illness; and the anticipated duration of the absence, if known.

Scope: Depending on the situation

## C0007

When an employee seeks leave for the first time for a FMLA-qualifying reason, the employee need not expressly assert rights under the FMLA or even mention the FMLA.

Scope: When an employee seeks leave for the first time for a FMLA-qualifying reason

## C0008

When an employee seeks leave due to a qualifying reason for which the employer has previously provided the employee FMLA-protected leave, the employee must specifically reference either the qualifying reason for leave or the need for FMLA leave.

Scope: When an employee seeks leave due to a qualifying reason for which the employer has previously provided the employee FMLA-protected leave

## C0013

When the need for leave is not foreseeable, an employee must comply with the employer's usual and customary notice and procedural requirements for requesting leave, absent unusual circumstances.

Scope: When the need for leave is not foreseeable, absent unusual circumstances

## C0015

If an employee requires emergency medical treatment, he or she would not be required to follow the call-in procedure until his or her condition is stabilized and he or she has access to, and is able to use, a phone.

Scope: If an employee requires emergency medical treatment, until his or her condition is stabilized and he or she has access to, and is able to use, a phone

## C0016

In the case of an emergency requiring leave because of a FMLA-qualifying reason, written advance notice pursuant to an employer's internal rules and procedures may not be required when FMLA leave is involved.

Scope: In the case of an emergency requiring leave because of a FMLA-qualifying reason when FMLA leave is involved

## C0017

If an employee does not comply with the employer's usual notice and procedural requirements, and no unusual circumstances justify the failure to comply, FMLA-protected leave may be delayed or denied.

Scope: If an employee does not comply with the employer's usual notice and procedural requirements, and no unusual circumstances justify the failure to comply

## Interpretation boundaries

C0000 preserves unforeseeability and practicable timing. C0002 preserves spokesperson eligibility. C0003 preserves the child emergency-treatment exemption. C0006 preserves the notice alternatives and their situation-dependent nature; do not require every branch to become a separate claim. C0007 and C0008 distinguish first from subsequent requests and preserve the either/or reference options. C0013 preserves unusual circumstances. C0015 preserves stabilization AND access AND ability to use a phone. C0016 preserves emergency and FMLA qualifications. C0017 preserves noncompliance AND absence of unusual circumstances.

C0014 summary is standalone permission with no scope; logic_text retains unusual-circumstances and emergency language but begins after the unforeseeability lead-in. Review each field separately. C0001 classification of generally-should-be-practicable, and C0004/C0010 expected-to-act modality, remain uncertain; do not treat the inventory/model label as normative truth.

A broad paragraph can support a judgment without proving it; inspect the specific source sentences and missing cross-passage lead-ins. The heading and section context can establish the document topic, but a claim that conditions appear explicitly in logic_text must be checked against that field.
