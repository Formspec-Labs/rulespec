# Masked source review before opening the arm key

Reviewed all statements and scopes, key alternatives and the target logic text
against the complete saved notice source and the constructed source sentences.
Row positions are zero-based. Labels are revisable engineering judgments, not
legal authority. The primary agent designed this experiment, so masking labels
does not create an independent review; the visible longer quotations also make
the intervention recognizable.

## Notice pair 1

Both R1 and R2 fail the standalone permission criterion. Row 14 has the same
unqualified designated-number statement and empty scope in both. R1's logic
retains the complete governing subsection, including unforeseeability; R2's
logic begins mid-sentence after that lead-in. Both retain unusual circumstances
and the emergency exception in logic. This is a selected-context difference,
not a repair of the statement.

Both retain timely notice, spokesperson permission conditional on inability,
the emergency asthma example, home-treatment timing, information alternatives,
first-time versus subsequent notice, insufficient “sick” notice, inquiries and
conditional denial, the general unforeseeable-leave duty with its exception,
all three emergency recovery prerequisites, written advance notice and the
limitation on delay/denial. R1 also includes unforeseeability in row 15's scope.

R1 labels the two “expected” clauses `not_stated`, R2 `should`. R1 labels the
written-notice “may not be required” clause `possible`, R2 `not_required`; the
prose is preserved in both. This is an unresolved classification difference,
with a potential loss of an explicit exemption label in R1. It is separate from
the target and not grounds to claim all source-force labels are equivalent.
R2's shorter information-choice field abbreviates the notified-of-impending-call
alternative, but its statement retains that branch and its logic quotes the
source. Count that as uneven field detail rather than complete meaning omission.

Both retain the remaining notice meanings and alternatives listed for pair 1,

R2 adds generic scopes to several notice-information records; no material
new dependency was identified in those additions.

## Constructed controls

Same-actor R1 and R2 both retain the signing duty and blue-pen permission as two
statements, without a waiver or invented precondition. Different-actor R1 and R2
both retain visitors' notice-on-arrival duty and staff's remote-work permission
without transferring the arrival condition. No lost meaning or false dependency
was identified in these simple controls.

## Masked decision

The standalone permission fails in all four notice outputs. Longer selected
context occurs in pair 1/R1 and pair 2/R1; only pair 2/R1 also improves the
scope field partially. Neither changes the target statement. The preregistered
semantic gate is unmet regardless of the arm key. Report context retention
separately from standalone meaning and preserve the classification differences.
