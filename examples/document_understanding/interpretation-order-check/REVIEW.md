# Blind source-review criteria

Written before inspecting any live output. Review every criterion against the
source and each relevant standalone statement; do not give missing statement
meaning credit merely because it appears in scope/choice/evidence. Record
pass/fail/uncertain and a short rationale with row numbers. Record interpretation
mistakes separately from statement mistakes. Results are revisable agent labels.

## Notice

- N1: The designated-number/person permission retains its unforeseeable-leave
  setting and unusual-circumstances qualification in the statement. If split into
  a standalone example, that example must retain them itself.
- N2: The emergency call-in exemption retains both stabilization and access to,
  and ability to use, a phone. Do not turn it into an unconditional exemption.
- N3: First-time FMLA notice does not require expressly asserting FMLA rights or
  mentioning FMLA; retain the first-time condition.
- N4: Previously provided FMLA-protected leave requires specifically referencing
  either the qualifying reason or the need for FMLA leave; preserve that setting
  and the alternative.
- N5: Preserve the qualified "generally should be practicable" expectation;
  do not turn it into an unconditional mandatory employer deadline.

## Waste

- W1: The accumulation permission retains quantity alternatives, location/control
  and the all-conditions exemption proviso.
- W2: The alternative compliance pathway retains exceptions (A)(7)/(A)(8), and
  incompatible-waste restrictions retain their remote compliance exceptions.
- W3: Closed-container exceptions retain adding/removing/consolidating and
  necessary temporary venting for either equipment operation or danger prevention.
- W4: Both the words Hazardous Waste AND a hazard indication are required.
- W5: Excess quantities trigger the three-consecutive-calendar-day duty, with
  compliance OR removal, and all three removal destinations retained.
- W6: Continued compliance and excess-date labeling retain the excess-accumulation
  case when split into separate statements.
- W7: Small-quantity and large-quantity preparedness/emergency requirements stay
  assigned to their respective generator categories.

## Constructed controls

- C1: The infant exception qualifies the badge duty, not map-lending permission.
- C2: Replacement requires a card lost over a month ago AND still unreported.
- C3: The temporary-pass permission retains both those parent conditions, imminent
  travel and the pending replacement.
- C4: Found-and-returned cards form a different branch. Cancellation stays should;
  do not import the unreported/over-a-month/imminent-travel conditions.
- C5: Employees and contractors are eligible categories, not a requirement that
  one person belong to both. Either a day pass or membership card suffices.
- C6: Two room windows remains descriptive; no duty or invented condition.

These synthetic controls partly overlap the extractor's built-in invented
demonstrations. They check obvious overcorrection and are not an independent
generalization test. The two real documents have also been used for development.
