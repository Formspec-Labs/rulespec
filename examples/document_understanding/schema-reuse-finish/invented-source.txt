The Permits Office states: This rule is in force from 2026-01-01T00:00:00Z until 2027-01-01T00:00:00Z.

Applicants aged at least 18 years must file within 30 days after receiving notice. They must supply either a signed declaration or a certified registration record. Applicants should retain a copy for 2 years after filing. Applicants receiving emergency assistance are not required to pay the filing fee; this exemption does not remove the requirement to supply supporting documents.

A certified registration record means a registration record bearing the registrar's certification.
