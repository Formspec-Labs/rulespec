# Field-specific source review

Freeze before calls. Judge each statement independently, even if another row,
an interpretation or a quotation retains its missing meaning. Record pass, fail
or uncertain with zero-based extraction row references. Statements are reviewed
under opaque IDs with experimental explanation fields removed, before reading
notes and arm labels. Existing scope/choice text remains visible as extracted
meaning but does not rescue an incomplete statement. Field descriptions and the
prior experiment's rubric are guidance, not assumed source truth.

## Passport checks

- PA1 (actor): IRL and IN modification prohibitions address agencies/centers;
  exceptional approval belongs to agency/center management, not posts.
- PA2 (actor): cleared-language duty and local-modification permission address
  posts, not agency/center management. Keep the two actors' rules distinct.
- PA3 (actor): full adjudication before suspension retains literal you or
  source-supported impersonal wording; do not invent a professional role.
- PM1 (modality): no applicant response and no personalization remain exemptions,
  rather than positive response duties or generic permissions.
- PM2 (modality): IRL/IN definitions and purpose remain descriptive; posts' index
  consultation/email contact remains permitted rather than mandatory.
- PL1 (logic): modification prohibitions retain central approval and the
  case-by-case management approval exception in extenuating circumstances.
- PL2 (logic): both index consultation and email contact survive; full adjudication
  precedes suspension and adjudication under 8 FAM precedes selecting the IRL.
- PS1 (applicability): if local modification is independently extracted, that
  permission retains local logistical/documentation limits. Do not generalize it
  to all actors, all content changes or the IN prohibition.

## Notice checks

- NA1 (actor): notice/providing information is the employee's responsibility;
  additional informal information gathering belongs to the employer; spokesperson
  substitution retains the employee's inability to act personally.
- NM1 (modality): the qualified generally-should-be-practicable expectation is
  retained without turning it into an unconditional deadline.
- NM2 (modality): first-time non-assertion, emergency phone exemption and explicit
  duty to answer employer questions retain their respective modal forces.
- NL1 (logic): repeat requests retain qualifying-reason OR FMLA-reference options;
  emergency calling waits for stabilization AND phone access/ability; possible
  denial for non-response retains inability to determine eligibility.
- NS1 (applicability, actual known miss): standalone designated-number/person
  permission retains unforeseeable-leave setting and unusual-circumstances limit.
- NS2 (applicability): first-time non-assertion and previously protected repeat
  leave retain their distinct governing settings.

Record separately, as uncertain rather than automatic pass/fail: how should/other
labels map to expected conduct, and whether may-not-be-required becomes too strong
when labeled not_required. These are known label uncertainties.

## Waste checks

- WA1 (actor): generator duties stay with the generator; small- versus large-
  quantity preparedness/emergency rules retain their categories.
- WM1 (modality): accumulation/compliance alternatives remain permission;
  containment/labeling/excess duties and incompatible-waste prohibitions retain
  their force and the incorporated-publication comment remains descriptive.
- WL1 (logic): both Hazardous Waste wording AND a hazard indication are required;
  illustrative hazard-label methods remain available without becoming cumulative
  obligations. Record missing illustrative methods as a separate completeness issue.
- WL2 (logic): container closure retains adding/removing/consolidating OR necessary
  temporary venting for equipment operation OR danger prevention.
- WL3 (logic): excess triggers three consecutive calendar days, compliance OR
  removal and all three removal destinations; do not change thresholds/units.
- WS1 (applicability): accumulation permission retains operator control/location
  and all-conditions proviso; later container/label rules retain their satellite-
  exemption setting when independently extracted.
- WS2 (applicability): split continued-compliance/date-label rules retain excess-
  accumulation setting; remote (A)(7)/(A)(8) and incompatible-waste exceptions
  remain qualified references rather than invented permissions.

## Explanation assessment after statement review

For each field/case report required-slot and non-null counts; substantive correct,
incomplete, unsupported, merely repetitive and uncertain examples; and whether
the matching statement preserves the explanation's qualifications. Measure gains
relative to that source's fresh baseline separately by field. Do not award a
single aggregate score across unequal fields or treat more notes as improvement.
