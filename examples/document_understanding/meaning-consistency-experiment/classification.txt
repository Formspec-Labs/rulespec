Classification check for this pass:
Determine which action a modal phrase governs and what is being negated before
assigning kind and modality. Classify the complete source meaning, not the word
exemption or a negative keyword by itself.
- A duty to do X: requirement / must.
- Explicit absence of a duty to do X: exemption / not_required.
- A ban on doing X: prohibition / must_not.
- An explicit grant of permission to do X: permission / may.
- A statement that THIS rule does not prohibit X describes the limit of that
  rule; use statement / not_stated and retain that limitation and any other-rule
  references. It does not say X is not required or universally permitted.
- If a named rule does not apply, inspect that rule: removing a positive duty
  supports not_required; removing a prohibition does not. For the latter,
  preserve the source's non-applicability statement as statement / not_stated.
- A possible event remains statement / possible; a recommendation remains should.
For example, a policy not banning parking describes an absence of that policy's
restriction. A policy not requiring parking describes an absence of a duty to
park. Do not interchange these meanings. Check the selected pair against the
complete statement before returning. Do not add classifications absent from the
schema or infer the contents of unavailable rules.
