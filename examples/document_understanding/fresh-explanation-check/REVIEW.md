# Frozen source criteria

Judge each independent affected statement against the source, not just whether
the text appears somewhere in the output. Splitting/merging is allowed when all
meaning is retained. A field enum may use a logically equivalent positive duty or
conditional prohibition; do not demand one surface form. Explicit permission,
recommendation and absence of duty must not become unconditional duties. Preserve
strict versus inclusive thresholds. Track omitted content and wrong additions.
Record pass/fail/uncertain, zero-based rows and a reason for each failure.

L below means a logic/scope criterion; M means a modal-force criterion. Some checks
test both. These labels only define which checks can support each field's gain;
every named check remains a regression guard for every arm.

## Oxygen — 14 CFR 91.211

- O1 [L]: general rules apply to civil aircraft of U.S. registry and use CABIN
  PRESSURE altitude. Keep that setting on the independently stated duties.
- O2 [L]: above 12,500 through and including 14,000 feet MSL; required minimum crew
  both provided oxygen AND using it for the portion exceeding 30 minutes, not all
  flight time or a rule triggered at exactly 12,500.
- O3 [L]: above 14,000 cabin pressure altitude, required minimum crew provided AND
  using oxygen for the entire time at those altitudes; no 30-minute allowance.
- O4 [L,M]: above 15,000 cabin pressure altitude, each occupant provided oxygen;
  do not invent a requirement that passengers use it.
- O5 [L]: pressurized U.S.-registry civil aircraft, FL250 flight-altitude threshold,
  at least 10 minutes for each occupant, additional to paragraph (a), for loss-of-
  pressurization descent. Do not confuse flight level and cabin pressure altitude.
- O6 [L]: above FL350 one pilot at controls wears/uses secured/sealed mask; either
  continuous supply OR automatic supply when cabin pressure exceeds 14,000.
- O7 [L,M]: mask-wearing exemption at or below FL410 retains above-FL350 pressurized
  setting, TWO pilots at controls and EACH has a quick-donning mask, one hand,
  ready position within five seconds, supplying oxygen and secured/sealed.
- O8 [L,M]: notwithstanding that exemption, above FL350 when one pilot leaves
  controls, remaining pilot must wear/use mask UNTIL the other returns to station.
  Do not preserve the two-pilot exemption while one pilot is absent.

## Alarms — 29 CFR 1910.165

- A1 [L]: employee-emergency alarm scope and exclusion of other discharge/
  supervisory systems unless intended as employee alarms; maintenance/testing/
  inspection nevertheless covers all local fire alarms used to alert employees.
- A2 [L]: pre-discharge employee alarms retain their specific referenced subset:
  (b)(1)-(4), (c), (d)(1); do not invent a universal exemption from all other laws.
- A3 [L,M]: warning can serve emergency action OR safe-escape reaction time OR both;
  tactile devices permitted for employees unable otherwise to recognize alarms.
- A4 [L]: telephone-number posting retains WHEN phones report emergencies; emergency
  message priority retains WHEN communication also serves as employee alarm.
- A5 [L,M]: direct voice permission and backup-system exemption retain <=10
  employees in that workplace AND all can hear; no blanket backup exemption.
- A6 [L,M]: approval requirement versus specified devices considered approved;
  illustrative alternative devices do not all become mandatory equipment.
- A7 [L]: operating-condition duty retains repair/maintenance exception; out-of-
  service backup means retain their trigger, not an always-applicable duty.
- A8 [L]: non-supervised systems tested every TWO MONTHS; multi-actuation systems
  use a different device per test, with no individual device in consecutive tests.
- A9 [L]: circuitry installed AFTER Jan 1 1981 AND capable of supervision must be
  supervised and notify assigned personnel of deficiencies; supervised systems
  separately tested AT LEAST annually, not every two months.
- A10 [L,M]: servicing/maintenance/testing requires persons trained in relevant
  operation/functions; manually operated devices unobstructed AND conspicuous AND
  readily accessible. These remain duties, not suggestions or alternatives.

## Procurement — 2 CFR 200.320

- P1 [M]: equitable micro-purchase distribution is SHOULD, qualified to extent
  practicable, among qualified suppliers; do not turn it into an absolute duty.
- P2 [L,M]: micro-purchase award without competitive quotations permitted only
  with reasonable-price judgment based on listed alternative information AND
  maintained supporting documents; purchase-card permission stays micro-purchase.
- P3 [L,M]: annual self-certification permission up to $50,000, maintain available
  documentation, justification AND identified threshold AND support for any ONE
  of three alternatives; State-law alternative limited to public institutions.
- P4 [L]: above $50,000 requires cognizant indirect-cost agency approval, request
  includes (a)(1)(iv) requirements, valid only until a relied-on factor changes.
- P5 [L,M]: simplified acquisitions above micro threshold and <= simplified
  threshold need adequate quotations; judgment of adequacy is permitted UNLESS
  agency specifies it. This agency limit also stays on sealed-bid adequacy judgment.
- P6 [L,M]: sealed-bid feasibility conditions SHOULD be present, all three retained:
  adequate specification, >=2 willing/able bidders, fixed-price suitability with
  selection principally by price. Do not upgrade this list to MUST or OR.
- P7 [L]: sealed-bid public invitation/public opening specifically for LOCAL
  governments, while all bids open at stated time/place; preserve scope of each.
- P8 [L]: sealed-bid cost factors considered WHEN specified in invitation; payment
  discounts used only when validity established from prior experience.
- P9 [M]: proposals may result in fixed-price OR cost-reimbursement contracts;
  they are descriptive possibilities, not a duty to award both or use one type.
- P10 [L,M]: public-response proposals considered to MAXIMUM EXTENT PRACTICABLE;
  selection considers price AND other factors, not lowest price alone.
- P11 [L,M]: qualifications-based price-excluded method only for architectural/
  engineering professional services, most qualified subject to fair/reasonable
  compensation negotiation; prohibition extends to other services by A/E firms.
- P12 [L,M]: noncompetitive procurement permitted only if at least one of FIVE
  alternatives applies. Written-request path requires request AND agency/pass-
  through written approval. Emergency requires inability to delay for public
  notice; inadequate competition follows solicitation of several sources.

For all sources, inspect other statements for missing sections, unsupported
assertions and enum/text disagreement. Report those separately so the checklist
does not hide failures. After statement review, inspect explanations for supported
interpretive value, repetition, omissions, new unsupported meaning, and whether
their qualifications survive in the matching statement.
